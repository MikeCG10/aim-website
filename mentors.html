Robert Velasco

Research Mentor

Graduate Technical Assistant researching cybersecurity frameworks with the NSA and NIST; CCDC and National Cyber League competitor; co-host of the Beyond the Horizon podcast.

I am a Graduate Technical Assistant at the Center for Cyber and AI and a Masters student pursuing a M.S. in Information Systems and Technology, Cybersecurity Concentration. I've completed research through the INSuRE course, partnering with the NSA and NIST to compare satellite networks to cybersecurity frameworks and test entropy for random number generators. I've participated in the Collegiate Cyber Defense Competition, with my main focus being policy & SIEM management. Similarly, I've participated in the National Cyber League twice and I look forward to participating again for this upcoming semester!

I'm also the co-host of the Beyond the Horizon podcast, where we talk about how AI affects differing industries and how important it is to acclimate yourself to it before it's too late. Beyond this, I hold both the CompTIA Security+ and CySA+ and am actively pursuing the Microsoft SC-200 certification and hope to pursue a career in Cybersecurity Analysis / Vulnerability Research!

Matthew Nguyen

Technical / Competition Mentor

Competes in CPTC, NCL, CCDC, and NCAE Cyber Games; teaches web exploitation and helps members break into offensive and defensive security.

I am an undergraduate student pursuing a B.S. in Information Systems and Technology with a concentration in Cybersecurity. I have participated in several cybersecurity competitions, including the NCAE Cyber Games, Collegiate Penetration Testing Competition (CPTC), National Cyber League (NCL), and Collegiate Cyber Defense Competition (CCDC). Through these experiences, I have gained hands-on experience in areas such as network defense, penetration testing, system administration, and securing services in competitive environments.

Outside of competitions, I enjoy helping others learn cybersecurity and sharing the knowledge I've gained through my own experiences. I have also helped teach web exploitation and enjoy introducing students to both offensive and defensive security concepts. I currently hold the CompTIA Security+ and Google Cloud Security certifications and am actively pursuing the CompTIA Network+ certification.

As an AIM mentor, I hope to help others strengthen their technical skills, get involved in cybersecurity competitions, and find opportunities that will help them succeed in the field. My long-term goal is to pursue a career in cybersecurity with a focus on both offensive and defensive security.
